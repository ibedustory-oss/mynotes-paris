Jjamppong in Paris

Where I have had jjamppong in Paris: Bulpan BBQ, Naderi, Bokko. All in the 15th.

What I thought

The place I've ordered from most is Bokko: five times on Uber Eats.

Bokko's is the spicy one. Best as a second stop after drinks.

Naderi's jjamppong is clean-tasting. The side dishes and the food are all clean-tasting!

At Bulpan, jjamppong with tangsuyuk makes a great pair.

Spicy: Bokko. Clean-tasting: Naderi. With tangsuyuk: Bulpan.
